    Gross estimate before taxes. No employee data is stored or transmitted by this app.

    Maximums depend on your policy

    Citywide DC 37 / CWA: 120 paid sick days. NYCPS administrative / OJ: half or one-third of at most 200 accumulated sick days, with a 100 paid-day ceiling (effective maximum 66⅔ days when dividing the 200-day balance by three). UFT administrative and teacher CAR: half of at most 200 days, maximum 100 paid days.

    NYCPS managerial: combined current and vested sick leave capped at 120 paid days. Current annual leave normally uses two years of accrual; approved excess carryover has a service-based maximum of three, four or five years. Separate vested annual leave is additional. Total payout is limited by final 12-month compensation, accounting for prior payments.

    For non-managerial annual and comp leave, use eligible, approved balances. A universal overall dollar cap has not been established for these presets. Custom and other groups require their own verified limits. Method B, uniformed, exempt-position and other special rules are not modeled.

    Policies, caps and sources (reviewed November 16, 2026)

    Union formulas reviewed November 16, 2026. Citywide Agreement, Article V Section 17(a),(e): 10 years of service, one-half before July 1, 2004 or one-third on/after that date, maximum 120 paid workdays. NYCPS administrative lump-sum rules: 10 years of service, minimum 2 or 3 sick days, maximum balance considered 200 days and maximum payment 100 days. NYCPS UFT administrative uses one-half regardless of hire date. These presets do not cover every union or title.

    This estimates retirement leave payouts, not pension income. City agencies, employee categories and union agreements can have different rules. Select the applicable Union policy to calculate sick leave; category labels alone do not establish coverage.

    Custom mode applies your entered payout percentages and limits. Annual limits apply to combined current and vested balances before the annual percentage. Sick limits apply to payable hours after the sick percentage. Comp time is included at your hourly rate. No eligibility or service requirement is checked in custom mode. Confirm all assumptions with your retirement office.

    The optional NYCPS preset retains administrative leave formulas from the original calculator. It is not a verified citywide rule. It assumes 7 hours per day, a hire date on or after July 1, 2004 and 25 annual accrual days unless changed. Service years are required for that preset. Pension calculations are excluded; the separate UFT teacher CAR option only estimates certified termination pay.
