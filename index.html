    Scope and calculation notes

    This estimates retirement leave payouts, not pension income. City agencies, employee categories and union agreements can have different rules. Category labels alone do not establish eligibility.

    Citywide mode applies your entered payout percentages and limits. Annual limits apply to combined current and vested balances before the annual percentage. Sick limits apply to payable hours after the sick percentage. Comp time is included at your hourly rate. No eligibility or service requirement is checked in custom mode. Confirm all assumptions with your retirement office.

    The optional NYCPS preset retains administrative leave formulas from the original calculator. It is not a verified citywide rule. It assumes 7 hours per day, a hire date on or after July 1, 2004 and 25 annual accrual days unless changed. Service years are required for that preset. It excludes pedagogical and pension calculations.
